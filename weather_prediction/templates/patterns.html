Weather Pattern Mining

Association rules (Apriori) discovered from discretized weather conditions

Continuous variables (humidity, pressure, cloud cover, temperature, wind speed, visibility) are discretized into Low / Moderate / High bins, then mined for combinations that reliably precede high rainfall, fog or heatwave events. Support = frequency of the pattern; Confidence = reliability of the rule; Lift > 1 means the combination is more predictive than chance.
